Interpretation:
Registration -> Triage is a common initial transition. Triage -> Doctor Consultation
is the most frequent adjacent transition. Doctor Consultation is followed by diagnostic
activities (Laboratory Test or X-Ray) in many sequences, and diagnostic activities may
be followed by another consultation. Treatment -> Discharge is a common terminal transition.
Repeated activities and loops are observable patterns; they are not, by themselves, proof
of unnecessary care, delay, or poor performance.

Method and limitations:
Events were sorted by case_id, event_timestamp, and event_id. A transition is counted
whenever one event is immediately followed by another event in the same case.
Complete sequence variants are exact ordered activity lists. This is descriptive
event-log analysis; it does not establish causation or clinical appropriateness.
PM4Py discovery was not executed in this environment.
